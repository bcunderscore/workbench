Hello world, because I wouldn't be a proper dev without including that somewhere in my first post.

I have wanted to rebuild my personal site for quite some time now. The old one was built when I was first getting into the software industry to have a dedicated place to showcase my personal projects and help me land my first job. However, I was lucky enough to acquire my first role before I could complete it, which led to the site being abandoned and remained uncompleted for many years.

So, it's time for a change...

In a world of AI generated slop, I have found that there is nothing quite like authentic, human craftsmanship and unfortunately, that seems to be a dwindling concept. I have been seriously inspired by makers like [matt3o](https://matt3o.com) to create this log and to share my work in a way that is both authentic and human and most importantly, free of AI writing and generated media. I know personally, I always get a breath of fresh air when consuming genuine human content, so I hope to provide the same to someone, somewhere. With that being said, now presenting:

## My Workbench

The design inspiration of this site comes from my own personal workbench which includes a [wrk. Mat](https://worklouder.cc/wrk-mat), a doubled sided, PVC cutting deskmat from *Work Louder* (no, I am not sponsored and that is not an affiliate link). I use this deskmat to build and assemble anything I can get my hands on.
